Privacy Policy: Color by Number: Cozy & Cute

Effective date: November 12, 2026
Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Color by Number: Cozy & Cute (the "App", package com.ganeshify.cozycolor), published by Ganeshify ("we", "us"), handles information when you use the App on Android.

The short version: the App has no accounts and no sign-in, and we do not ask for your name, email, contacts, camera, microphone or precise location. Your coloring progress stays on your phone. The App shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects device and advertising data as described below.

1. Information handled by the App

a) Stored only on your device

  • Your coloring progress (which areas you filled and in what order), finished pictures, settings (sound, vibration), hint count and unlocked pictures. These are kept in the App's private storage on your phone.
  • Small thumbnail images of your progress and pictures you have opened, so they work offline.

We do not receive or upload this information. It is deleted when you clear the App's data or uninstall it.

b) Downloaded content
To show new pictures, the App downloads a picture list and picture files from our content server, hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). The App also downloads a small advertising settings file from a Google Apps Script endpoint that we control. These requests do not include personal information from the App. Like any web server, these providers may record technical data such as your IP address, the time of the request and your device's browser/network details, under their own privacy policies.

c) Advertising
The App shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Where required, the App asks for your consent with Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Google may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version);
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • ad interactions (impressions, clicks, rewarded ad completions) and diagnostic data from the ads SDK.

Google uses this data to deliver, measure and personalise ads (where permitted), and to prevent fraud. See "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services": https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

d) Saving and sharing your pictures
When you tap Save to gallery, the App writes your finished picture to your device's photo gallery. When you tap Share, Android's share sheet sends the picture to the app you choose. We do not receive these pictures.

2. Information we do not request

The App does not request or collect: your name, email address or phone number; accounts or passwords; contacts; camera or microphone; precise GPS location; payment information (there are no in-app purchases); or the contents of your photo gallery.

3. How information is used

The information above is used to: show pictures and keep your progress; load new picture packs; decide which ad formats are switched on; show ads and rewarded ads; prevent fraud and abuse; and comply with law.

4. Service providers and sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is processed by these providers:

Provider Purpose Policy
Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform Ads, consent, fraud prevention https://policies.google.com/privacy
Google Apps Script Ad settings file https://policies.google.com/privacy
GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) Hosting the picture catalog and images https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights, users or the public.

5. Data retention

On-device data stays until you clear the App's data or uninstall it. We keep no copy. Data collected by Google or GitHub is kept according to their own retention policies.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Ad consent: where required, you can change your consent at any time in Settings → Privacy options in the App.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Delete your data: clear the App's storage or uninstall it.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU/EEA, UK, California or India), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of personal data. Because we do not hold personal data about you on our servers, most requests concern the providers above. Contact us and we will help.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

8. Data security

Network traffic uses HTTPS. On-device data is stored in the App's private storage. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

9. International data transfers

Google and GitHub may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States, under their own safeguards.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this policy. The updated version will be published at the same location with a new "Last updated" date. Significant changes may also be announced in the App.
